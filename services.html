What We Offer

Our Staffing Services

Tailored solutions designed to meet every school's unique health staffing needs.

🕐

Per Diem Staffing

Same-day and next-day coverage for unexpected absences. Our on-call pool of credentialed school nurses is available 365 days a year.

📅

Long-Term Placements

Semester or year-long assignments for extended leaves, FMLA coverage, or districts building their health program.

🏠

Permanent Placement

Direct-hire matching for districts seeking to grow their permanent school nursing team. We source, screen, and present the best candidates.

☀️

Summer Programs

Qualified nurses for summer school, day camps, and extended care programs. Short-term, flexible engagements available.

💜

Special Education Support

Nurses trained in IEP/504 plan compliance, G-tube feeding, seizure protocols, and other specialized student health needs.

📋

Compliance & Credentialing

We manage background checks, TB tests, CPR/AED certification, and state licensure verification for every nurse we place.
