Our Story

About Tempositions

Dedicated to student health for over 15 years. We believe every child deserves a qualified nurse on campus, every school day.

Our Mission

Every Student Deserves a Nurse

Our mission is simple: ensure that every student has access to quality healthcare at school. We bridge the critical gap between schools in need and experienced nurses who care deeply about the communities they serve.

We partner with school districts, charter schools, and private institutions to provide seamless, compliant, and compassionate nursing coverage — so administrators can focus on education, and nurses can focus on students.

"When a student is sick, scared, or injured, they need immediate care from a qualified professional. We exist to make sure that person is always there."

Our Journey

How We Got Here

From a single school district to nationwide reach — our growth mirrors the trust schools place in us.

2009

Founded in California

Tempositions was founded by a group of school administrators and nursing professionals who saw a critical gap in school health staffing support.

2013

Expanded to 5 States

Our reputation for compliance and speed earned us contracts across California, New York, Texas, Illinois, and Florida.

2017

Launched Special Education Division

We created a specialized team dedicated to placing nurses trained in medically complex student needs and IEP compliance.

2021

Nationwide Reach

Now operating in 28 states, Tempositions serves over 500 school districts and has placed more than 1,200 nurses.

Today

Growing Every Day

We continue to expand our network, adding new nurses and partnering with new districts who share our commitment to student health.
