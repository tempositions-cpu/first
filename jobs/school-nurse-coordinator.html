School Nurse Coordinator

📍 Dallas, TX Full-Time Leadership Role
$80,000–95,000 / year
Apply Now ← Back to Jobs

Job Overview

Tempositions is recruiting an experienced School Nurse Coordinator for a dynamic and growing school district in the Dallas-Fort Worth metro area. This is a district-level leadership position overseeing school health programs across 12 campuses, serving a combined student population of over 9,500 students.

This role is ideal for a seasoned school nurse who is ready to step into a leadership capacity, shaping health policy, mentoring nursing staff, and driving district-wide wellness initiatives. You'll work directly with district administration, school principals, and community health partners.

🌟 Leadership Opportunity

This is one of the most impactful school nursing roles in the DFW area. As Coordinator, you'll directly shape the health of 9,500+ students and guide a team of campus nurses across 12 schools.

Responsibilities

  • Supervise and provide clinical guidance to a team of 12 campus school nurses
  • Develop, implement, and evaluate district-wide school health policies and procedures
  • Ensure compliance with Texas Education Agency (TEA) health requirements and state law
  • Coordinate communicable disease surveillance and reporting
  • Lead district health screenings program (vision, hearing, spinal) and data reporting
  • Oversee student health records management and HIPAA compliance across all campuses
  • Serve as the district liaison to local health departments, community agencies, and TEA
  • Provide professional development and training for nursing staff and health clerks
  • Collaborate with special education department on health-related IEP and 504 components
  • Manage the district health services budget and supply procurement
  • Support hiring, onboarding, and performance evaluation of campus nurses

Requirements

  • Active, unencumbered Texas RN license in good standing
  • BSN required; MSN strongly preferred
  • Minimum 5 years school nursing experience, with at least 2 years in a lead or supervisory role
  • Texas School Nurse certification or willingness to obtain within 1 year
  • Current BLS/CPR certification
  • Texas DPS fingerprint clearance
  • Strong knowledge of Texas Education Code health requirements and TEA guidelines
  • Experience with student health information systems (preferred: Frontline Health or similar)
  • Demonstrated leadership, communication, and organizational skills

Benefits

  • Competitive salary: $80,000–$95,000/year (commensurate with experience)
  • Comprehensive health, dental, and vision benefits
  • Texas Teacher Retirement System (TRS) pension contribution
  • District vehicle or mileage reimbursement for multi-campus travel
  • Generous PTO, paid holidays, and winter/spring breaks
  • Annual professional development budget ($2,500)
  • Strong administrative support and collaborative leadership team
  • Opportunity to build and shape a district health program from the ground up

About the District

This rapidly growing DFW-area district has added three new campuses in the past four years and continues to expand. District leadership is deeply committed to student health and wellness, recently approving a dedicated health services budget increase and a new district-wide health information system.

The district is known for its strong community relationships, diverse student population, and a culture of innovation. This role reports directly to the Assistant Superintendent of Student Services and has a seat at the leadership table for district-wide planning.
