Apply Now ← Back to Jobs

Job Overview

Tempositions is actively seeking licensed Registered Nurses (RNs) for per diem school nursing assignments throughout the Los Angeles Unified School District and surrounding districts. This is an excellent opportunity for experienced RNs who want flexible scheduling while making a meaningful impact on student health and well-being.

As a per diem school nurse, you will provide health services to students and staff, manage health records, administer medications, and respond to medical emergencies across K-12 school settings.

Responsibilities

  • Provide direct nursing care to students, including first aid, illness assessment, and emergency response
  • Administer medications and treatments per physician orders and school policy
  • Maintain accurate student health records in compliance with HIPAA and state regulations
  • Communicate with parents, teachers, and administrators regarding student health concerns
  • Monitor and manage students with chronic conditions (asthma, diabetes, epilepsy, allergies)
  • Conduct basic vision and hearing screenings as required
  • Support compliance with IEP and 504 health plans
  • Document all nursing activities in the school's health information system

Requirements

  • Active, unencumbered California RN license in good standing
  • Current CPR/AED certification (BLS)
  • Negative TB test within the last 12 months (or IGRA blood test)
  • Clear DOJ and FBI background check
  • Minimum 1 year of nursing experience (school, pediatric, or ER experience preferred)
  • Proficiency in electronic health records (EHR)
  • Strong communication and interpersonal skills
  • Ability to work independently in a school setting

Benefits

  • Competitive pay: $55–65/hour depending on experience
  • Weekly direct deposit paychecks
  • Flexible scheduling — you choose when you work
  • Access to assignments across multiple districts and campuses
  • Dedicated Tempositions support team available throughout each assignment
  • Opportunity to convert to long-term or permanent placement
  • Professional development and continuing education resources

About the District

Los Angeles Unified School District (LAUSD) is the second-largest school district in the United States, serving approximately 575,000 students across more than 900 schools in Los Angeles County. LAUSD is committed to the health and wellness of all students and employs a comprehensive school nursing program.

Our nurses work in a variety of school environments — elementary, middle, and high schools — and enjoy the support of a dedicated health services team.
