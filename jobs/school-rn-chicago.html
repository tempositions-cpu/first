School Registered Nurse (RN)

📍 Chicago, IL Full-Time
$75,000–85,000 / year
Apply Now ← Back to Jobs

Job Overview

Tempositions is partnering with a growing Chicago-area school district to place a full-time Registered Nurse for the upcoming academic year. This permanent placement offers a competitive salary, comprehensive benefits, and the opportunity to build lasting relationships with students, families, and staff in a supportive school community.

The district serves approximately 2,400 students across three campuses. You will be the primary health professional at one campus, with district health leadership support and collaboration with nurses at the other campuses.

Responsibilities

  • Serve as the campus school nurse for an elementary or middle school campus
  • Provide first aid, illness assessment, injury triage, and emergency response
  • Administer scheduled and as-needed medications per physician orders
  • Maintain and update student health records in compliance with Illinois state law and HIPAA
  • Develop and implement individualized health plans (IHPs) for students with chronic conditions
  • Conduct mandated health screenings (vision, hearing, BMI, scoliosis)
  • Collaborate with special education staff on nursing components of IEPs and 504 plans
  • Provide health education to students, staff, and families
  • Participate in district health committee meetings and professional development

Requirements

  • Active, unencumbered Illinois RN license in good standing
  • BSN required; MSN preferred
  • School Nurse Endorsement (or willingness to obtain within 2 years)
  • Current BLS/CPR certification
  • Fingerprint-based background clearance
  • Minimum 3 years nursing experience; school or pediatric nursing strongly preferred
  • Experience with student health information systems (e.g., Infinite Campus, PowerSchool Health)
  • Strong organizational and communication skills

Benefits

  • Competitive salary: $75,000–$85,000/year (commensurate with experience)
  • Comprehensive health, dental, and vision insurance
  • Illinois Teachers' Retirement System (TRS) pension contribution
  • Generous paid time off and paid school holidays
  • Annual professional development stipend ($1,000)
  • Supportive district administration with strong school nursing program
  • Summer break schedule (10-month employment with options for summer work)

About the District

This is a highly regarded suburban Chicago school district known for its commitment to student wellness and strong academic programming. The district has invested significantly in its school health program over the past five years, including updated health office facilities at each campus and a district-wide electronic health record system.

The community is tight-knit and supportive, with active parent organizations and a school board that prioritizes whole-child education. Nurses in this district report high job satisfaction and strong administrative backing.
